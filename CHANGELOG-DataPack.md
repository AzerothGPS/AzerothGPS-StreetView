# Changelog: AzerothGPS StreetView DataPack

The street view pictures for AzerothGPS StreetView, versioned by date. Each section is that version's release
notes on CurseForge and GitHub (`sv.cmd release-data`).

## 2026.10.03

The first DataPack! All of AzerothGPS StreetView's pictures, now a download of their own so that updates of
StreetView itself stay small.

- **1,873 street views**, each a full 360-degree panorama:
  - the roads of the Eastern Kingdoms, Kalimdor and Zephras Isle, about one every 200 yards;
  - the streets and halls of Stormwind, Orgrimmar, Ironforge, Darnassus, Thunder Bluff and the Undercity, and
    inside their buildings;
  - a few dozen famous spots, each framed on its best view;
  - **every dungeon and raid boss**, 205 of them in 26 dungeons and raids, from Ragefire Chasm to Naxxramas.
- **Wildlife** along the roads: the creatures that live there, standing about the way you'd meet them.
- Needs AzerothGPS StreetView 1.0.0 or later (the CurseForge app installs this with it).
