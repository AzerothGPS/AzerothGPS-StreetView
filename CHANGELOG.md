# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Street views on the AzerothGPS map: drag the figure onto a road to look around there; a
  plain click opens the street view nearest you.
- The viewer draws each spot in true perspective: drag to look around, the mouse wheel zooms,
  the arrow buttons turn and tilt. White arrows on the ground lead along the roads to the next
  street views.
- The spot and the way you look show on the AzerothGPS map.
- The pictures come in one pack per continent, installed with the viewer.
