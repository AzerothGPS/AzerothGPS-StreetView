# Multiplayer

Everyone in the game needs AzerothGPS StreetView. The host picks the street views and keeps the rounds
moving, and each player scores their own guess and shares it with the others.

## Playing with others

Pick **Link** (then a level and the rounds) to host a game. Nothing gets sent until you send it:

1. The panel shows **Send the link:** with buttons for Party, Raid, Say, Guild, your numbered chat channels
   (General, Trade...) and **Target** (a whisper to the player you have targeted). Click one and the game's
   link goes into your chat box on that chat, the way linking a map position does. Press Enter to send it.
   Already typing a message? The link goes right where your cursor is. (Once every 5 seconds, so nobody
   gets spammed.)
2. Players with StreetView see **[Join Where in the Azeroth?: Heroic, 3 rounds]** in chat, so they know the
   level and the rounds before they click. One click and they're in, no popups. Players without the addon
   just see a short code.
3. Up to **40 players** can join. The game starts when the lobby's countdown ends, when it's full, or
   when you click **Start now**.

Link games work on your realm and faction (they run on a hidden chat channel of their own). Click too
late and you're told the game already started, is full, or is over.

## The lobby

Every multiplayer game counts down **30 seconds** before the first round ("starts in" under the title).
Everyone sees the same countdown, no matter when they joined.

## The player list

![The player list: who has guessed](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f829c029-1d69-4029-a017-9db19214420b)

Once the rounds start, the player list sits in the street view's corner, right under the timer:

- Players are ranked by total, **five at a time**. Scroll the **mouse wheel** over the list to see the
  rest; a thin bar shows where you are.
- **Your own row** stays pinned under the list when you're further down.
- During a round, players show **guessed** as soon as they place a guess. Nobody sees where, or how many
  points, until the round's result.
- **Hover a player's marker** on the map (between rounds or at the end) to see their name, their points
  and distance for the round, and their total.

In the lobby, or if you close the street view, the list shows on the map's panel instead.

## Leaving, and /reload

- A player who leaves (the X, leaving the party, going offline) drops off the list, and the game carries
  on without them.
- If the host leaves or goes quiet, the game ends for everyone.
- A `/reload` doesn't count as leaving. You come right back into the game and catch up on what you missed.
