# Multiplayer

Everyone in a game needs AzerothGPS StreetView. The host picks the street views and paces the rounds;
each player scores their own guess and shares it.

## Party and raid

Choose **Party**: everyone in your party or raid is asked to join. The game starts when everyone has
answered, when the host clicks **Start now**, or when the 30-second lobby runs out.

## Whisper

Choose **Whisper** and give a name: your target, a typed name (first and last, as WoW Forever shows it),
or shift-click their name in chat. They're asked to join.

## Link (open games)

Choose **Link** to host an open game anyone can join:

1. The panel shows **Post the link:** with buttons for Say, Guild, Party, Raid and your numbered chat
   channels (General, Trade...). Click one to post (once every 5 seconds).
2. Players with StreetView see **[Join Where in the Azeroth?]** in chat. Clicking it joins, no questions
   asked. Players without the addon just see a short code.
3. Up to **40 players** join; the game starts when the lobby's countdown ends, when it's full, or when
   the host clicks **Start now**.

Link games work on your realm and faction (they use a hidden chat channel of their own). A player who
clicks too late is told the game already started, is full, or is over.

## The lobby

Every multiplayer game counts down **30 seconds** before the first round ("starts in" under the title).
Everyone sees the same countdown, whenever they joined.

## The scoreboard

- The players by total, **five at a time**; the **mouse wheel** over the list scrolls through the rest,
  with a thin bar showing where you are.
- **Your own row** stays pinned under the list when you're lower down.
- During a round the others show **guessed** as soon as they place a guess, never where or how many
  points: those show with the round's result.
- **Hover a player's marker** on the map (between rounds or at the end) for their name, the round's
  points and distance, and their total.

## Leaving

- A player who leaves (the X, leaving the party, going offline) drops off the list; the game goes on.
- If the host leaves or goes silent, the game ends for everyone.
