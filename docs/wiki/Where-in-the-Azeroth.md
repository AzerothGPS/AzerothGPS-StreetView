# Where in the Azeroth?

A GeoGuessr-style game on the AzerothGPS map. You see a street view with no zone name or coordinates;
look around, then guess where it is by double-clicking the map. The closer your guess, the more points.

## Starting a game

Click the **Where in the Azeroth?** button above the map's figure. A menu slides out:

| Mode | Who plays |
|---|---|
| **Solo** | Just you. Your average round score counts. |
| **Party** | Everyone in your party or raid who has StreetView is asked to join. |
| **Whisper** | One player: your target, a name you type, or shift-click their name in chat. |
| **Link** | An open game: post its link in chat; whoever clicks it joins (up to 40). See [[Multiplayer]]. |

Then pick **1, 3 or 5 rounds**. The menu closes by itself if you leave it alone for 20 seconds.

## A round

1. A street view opens with a **30-second** countdown on the picture.
2. Look around (drag, mouse wheel).
3. **Double-click the map** to place your guess. Double-click again to move it; the one placed when the
   time runs out counts. Solo, you can **Submit guess** early.
4. When the time is up the map pans to the answer: a dotted line grows from your guess to the real spot,
   with everyone's guesses in a multiplayer game.
5. **10 seconds** later the next round starts ("next round in" counts down under the title).

The street view stays up after the round so you can look again; **Show Street View** reopens it if you
closed it.

## Scoring

Each round is worth 0 to 100 points:

| Distance | Points |
|---|---|
| within 25 yards | 100 |
| 200 yards | 92 |
| 500 yards | 78 |
| 1,000 yards | 58 |
| 2,000 yards | 30 |
| 4,000 yards | 7 |
| another continent, or no guess | 0 |

Being in the right zone isn't enough for full marks: the points fall quickly past a few hundred yards.

**Ties:** players with the same points are told apart by who was closer. The scoreboard then shows
decimals, for example 99.8 for a guess 5 yards off and 99.6 for one 10 yards off. The same goes for
the totals.

An average of 75 or more earns a celebration at the end.

## What you'll see

- Open world and cities only: never dungeons or caves.
- Only street views every player has (if someone has an older version, the panel says who lacks what).
- During the game the map hides your route, dungeons and transports (boats, zeppelins) so they give
  nothing away. When the game ends the map goes back to what you were looking at, and your route comes back.

The panel's **X** leaves the game (the host's ends it for everyone). A finished game closes by itself
after a minute.
