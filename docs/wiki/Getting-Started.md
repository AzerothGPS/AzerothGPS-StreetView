# Getting Started

## Install

1. Grab [AzerothGPS](https://github.com/AzerothGPS/AzerothGPS) first. StreetView needs it, and a recent
   one: the game's levels need AzerothGPS 1.1 or newer.
2. Install **AzerothGPS StreetView** from CurseForge, or unzip it into
   `World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns`.
3. Install its pictures, **AzerothGPS StreetView Data** (about 1.2 GB), the same way. They're a download
   of their own so that StreetView's updates stay small, and they update separately when new street
   views come out. Without them, StreetView shows a note saying where to get them.
4. Restart the game completely. New picture files only load on a full start, not on a `/reload`.

## Your first look around

1. Open the AzerothGPS map.
2. Drag the little figure (it sits next to the map's buttons) onto the map. The roads light up, the one
   under your pointer brightest, and the street views show up as dots.
3. Drop it on a road and the nearest street view opens in its own window.

Just want to see where you're standing? Click the figure (or type `/sv here`) for the street view
nearest you.

## Your first game

1. Click the **Where in the Azeroth?** button above the figure.
2. Pick **Solo**, then **Solo** again (or **Against Bots** if you want some company), a level (Normal is
   a good start) and 1, 3 or 5 rounds.
3. A street view opens. Look around, then **double-click the map** where you think it is.
4. When the 30 seconds are up, the map shows you the real spot and how many points you got.

The rules are on [[Where in the Azeroth?|Where-in-the-Azeroth]], and [[Multiplayer]] covers playing
with friends.
