# Getting Started

## Install

1. Install [AzerothGPS](https://github.com/AzerothGPS/AzerothGPS) (StreetView needs it; a current version:
   the game uses its newer map API).
2. Install **AzerothGPS StreetView** from CurseForge (or unzip it into
   `World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns`). The street views come inside the addon: there's
   nothing else to download.
3. Restart the game completely (new picture files load only on a full start, not on `/reload`).

## Your first street view

1. Open the AzerothGPS map.
2. Drag the little figure (next to the map's buttons) onto the map: the roads light up, the one under
   the pointer brightest, and the street views show as dots.
3. Drop it on a road: the nearest street view opens in its own window.

A plain click on the figure (or `/sv here`) opens the street view nearest you.

## Your first game

1. Click the **Where in the Azeroth?** button above the figure.
2. Choose **Solo**, then 1, 3 or 5 rounds.
3. A street view opens: look around, then **double-click the map** where you think it is.
4. When the 30 seconds are up, the answer shows with your points.

See [[Where in the Azeroth?|Where-in-the-Azeroth]] for the rules, and [[Multiplayer]] to play with others.
