# Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/sv` | Reminds you how to open a street view (drag the figure from the AzerothGPS map onto a road). |
| `/sv here` | Opens the street view nearest you. |
| `/sv open <id>` | Opens a street view by its id. |
| `/sv list` | Lists every installed street view (that's a lot of lines). |
| `/sv hide` | Closes the viewer. |
| `/sv flipyaw` | Turns the views the other way, if left and right look swapped. |
| `/sv probe` | Checks that your game client can show the addon's JPEG pictures. |

Where in the Azeroth? doesn't need a command: just click its button above the figure on the AzerothGPS
map.
