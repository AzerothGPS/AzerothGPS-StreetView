# Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/sv` | How to open a street view (drag the figure from the AzerothGPS map onto a road). |
| `/sv here` | The street view nearest you. |
| `/sv open <id>` | A street view by its id. |
| `/sv list` | The installed street views. |
| `/sv hide` | Close the viewer. |
| `/sv flipyaw` | Turn the views the other way (if left and right look swapped). |
| `/sv probe` | Check that your game client shows the addon's JPEG pictures. |

Where in the Azeroth? is started from its button above the AzerothGPS map's figure.
