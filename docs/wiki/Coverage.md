# Coverage

## Now (1.0.0)

- **1,407 street views** along the roads of the open world, about one every 200 yards:
  - the Eastern Kingdoms
  - Kalimdor
  - Zephras Isle
- Every picture is a full 360-degree panorama, looking up and down too.

## Coming

- **Cities:** street views inside the capitals (they'll be in the game too).
- **Dungeons:** key spots only: the entrance, the bosses and the stairs between floors (in the viewer,
  never in the game).

## Pictures that look wrong

Some zones have strong colors of their own (the pink light of Winterspring, the orange haze of the
Plaguelands). If a picture looks broken instead (black patches, the view inside a wall or a tree, the
ground missing), please report its id (the window's title shows where it is) on the project's issue page.
