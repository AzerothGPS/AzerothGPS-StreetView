# Street View

## Opening a street view

- **Drag the figure** from the AzerothGPS map onto a road. While you drag, the road network lights up
  (the road under the pointer brightest) and the street views show as dots. Drop it to open the nearest.
- **Click the figure**, or type `/sv here`, for the street view nearest you.
- `/sv open <id>` opens one by its id (`/sv list` lists them).

## Looking around

- **Drag the picture** to look around: the scenery follows the pointer, up and down too.
- **Mouse wheel** zooms in and out.
- **White arrows** on the ground point along the roads and toward the next street views. Click one to
  walk that way. They hide while you drag.
- The window's title shows the zone and map coordinates. Drag the title bar to move the window and
  the corner to resize it. Escape closes it.

## On the map

While a street view is open, the AzerothGPS map shows where it is and which way you're looking, and
updates as you turn or walk.

## Tips

- Pictures are 360-degree panoramas rendered from the game world, a spot about every 200 yards along the
  roads.
- If left and right look swapped on your client, `/sv flipyaw` turns the views the other way.
