## Description

[image: header, the Where in the Azeroth? logo (assets/where-in-the-azeroth.png)]

### See Azeroth from the road

**AzerothGPS StreetView** adds street-level views to the [AzerothGPS](https://github.com/AzerothGPS/AzerothGPS)
map. Drag the little figure off the map onto a road and a full 360-degree view of that spot opens:
look around, look up, zoom in, and follow the arrows on the ground to walk along the road.

[image: GIF, dragging the figure onto a road and the view opening]

- **1,407 street views** along the roads of the Eastern Kingdoms, Kalimdor and Zephras Isle, about
  one every 200 yards.
- **Full panoramas:** drag to look in any direction, the mouse wheel zooms.
- **Walk the roads:** click the white arrows on the ground to move to the next street view.
- **On the map:** AzerothGPS shows where you are looking from and which way you face.
- **Everything included:** the pictures ship inside the addon. No extra downloads.

[image: a street view window, a forest road]

### Where in the Azeroth?

A guessing game on your map. You get a street view with no zone name and no coordinates: look around,
then double-click the map where you think it is. The closer you are, the more points you score.

[image: GIF, a round from the street view to the answer's dotted line]

- **Play solo, or with friends:** your party or raid, one player by whisper, or an **open game** anyone
  can join from a link you post in chat, **up to 40 players**.
- **1, 3 or 5 rounds**, 30 seconds each, with a countdown on the picture.
- **Scores from 0 to 100** a round: 100 within 25 yards, and they drop fast, so knowing the zone isn't
  enough. The closer guess wins a tie.
- **A live scoreboard** that shows who has guessed, never where, until the round's result. Hover the
  markers on the map to see who guessed what.
- **Fair:** the map hides your route, dungeons and transports while you play; only open-world and city
  street views are used, and only ones every player has.

[image: the final results, several players' guesses on the map with the scoreboard]

### Getting started

1. Install **AzerothGPS** and **AzerothGPS StreetView**, then restart the game.
2. Open the AzerothGPS map and drag the figure onto a road.
3. Click **Where in the Azeroth?** above the figure to play.

Commands: `/sv here` (the street view nearest you), `/sv list`, `/sv hide`. The full guide is on the
wiki.
